Which grades does Dalmondeor offer?+

Dalmondeor Primary supports learners from Grade R through Grade 7.

How can I enquire about admission?+

Call the school on 010 030 0164. The team can confirm current requirements, dates and availability.

Where is the school located?+

The school is situated in Mondeor, Johannesburg South. Use the map link below for directions.

How can parents stay updated?+

Follow the official Dalmondeor Dallies Facebook page and the school’s parent communication channels.
